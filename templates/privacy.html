TRUST & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and professional confidentiality are paramount. Here is a clear, transparent breakdown of how CareerVerse AI handles data.

Last Updated: August 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Zero Sale of Personal Data

CareerVerse AI operates as a 100% free and open career intelligence ecosystem. We do not sell, monetize, rent, or lease your personal information, job queries, or resume contents to any third-party advertisers or recruitment agencies.

2. In-Memory Resume Processing

When you upload your PDF resume to our ATS Resume Analyzer:

  • Your file is processed dynamically in volatile server memory using pdfplumber purely to extract raw skill vectors and project text.
  • Your uploaded PDF is never stored on permanent disk storage or retained after the analysis response is returned.
  • Your resume text is never used to train public machine learning foundation models.

🔒 Zero Disk Retention: Once your resume evaluation score and bullet point suggestions are generated, the raw document buffer is discarded immediately.

3. AI Query Interactions

When you use the AI Roadmap Generator, Career Chat, or Market Intelligence tools, your role inputs (e.g., "AI Engineer", "Data Scientist") and question prompts are forwarded securely to Google's enterprise Gemini AI endpoints to generate real-time evaluations.

  • Prompts are processed over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.
  • We do not store your private chat dialogues on public database servers.
  • Client-side chat history is stored locally in your browser's localStorage / sessionStorage for your own convenience.

4. Cookies & Session Storage

CareerVerse AI does not use tracking cookies or invasive cross-site behavioral monitoring. We use minimal functional cookies and browser storage strictly required for theme preferences and session integrity.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. Because we do not mandate account creation or store long-term user dossiers, clearing your browser cache and cookies immediately removes all traces of previous searches and tool outputs from your client device.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions, compliance requests, or privacy suggestions, please contact our team via the Support Center.
