Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareerVerse AI completely free to use?
Yes! All 10 AI tools, customized roadmaps, PDF resume evaluations, and compensation predictors are 100% free and open access for students and professionals worldwide.
How are the salary benchmarks calculated?
Our salary engine combines real-world hiring market data from tech hubs in India, USA, UK, Germany, Canada, and 195+ countries with Google's Gemini AI to deliver verified fresher, mid-level, and senior pay bands.
Is my resume stored on your servers?
No. Your uploaded PDF resume is parsed strictly in temporary memory to calculate your ATS match score and bullet point enhancements. It is never saved to permanent disk storage or shared.
Can I request a custom career roadmap that isn't listed?
Yes! Our Universal Career Validation Engine accepts any legitimate job title, trade, scientific domain, or emerging tech role across any global sector.
